Career Page
FC Barcelona (2004-2021)
- 778 official matches, 672 goals — the club's all-time top scorer by a wide margin
- 10 La Liga titles · 4 UEFA Champions League titles · 7 Copa del Rey titles
- Debuted at 17 in 2004; became captain in 2018
- Defining moment: his 2009 Champions League final performance and the 2011-12 season, in which he scored
91 goals across club and country — a Guinness World Record for a single calendar year
Paris Saint-Germain (2021-2023)
- Two Ligue 1 titles
- Joined after Barcelona's financial situation made re-signing him impossible
- Defining moment: while at PSG, captained Argentina to the 2022 World Cup title in Qatar
Inter Miami CF (2023-present)
- Won the Leagues Cup in his first month with the club (2023)
- MLS Cup champion (2025) — the club's first major trophy
- MLS MVP (2024 and 2025) and MLS Golden Boot (2025)
- Credited with a measurable surge in MLS attendance, viewership, and merchandise sales — the "Messi effect"
Argentina National Team (debut 2005)
- FIFA World Cup champion, 2022
- Copa América champion, 2021 and 2024
- Olympic gold medalist, Beijing 2008
- Argentina's all-time leading scorer (125+ goals)
- At the 2026 World Cup, Messi — in what's expected to be his final World Cup appearance — became the tournament's all-time leading scorer across his career, with 21 goals since his 2006 debut, and the first player ever to score in seven consecutive World Cup matches. Argentina reached the final as defending champions but were beaten 1-0 after extra time by Spain; Messi collected the runner-up medal to an emotional ovation.
