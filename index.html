THE JOURNEY OF A LEGEND
Born in Rosario,Argentina in 1987,Messi showed extraordinary talent early -talent that nearly went undeveloped when a growth hormone condition put his future in football at risk.FC Barcelona's La Masia academy took him in at 13, treatment and all.Everything that followed- the records,the trophies,the world cup-traces back to that decision.
